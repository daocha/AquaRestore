# AquaRestore support

**English** | [繁體中文](SUPPORT.zh_TW.md)

AquaRestore restores the colors of underwater videos and photos on Android and iPhone. This page explains how to get
help with the app.

## Contact

Open an [issue](https://github.com/daocha/AquaRestore/issues) to ask a question, report a bug or suggest an
improvement. Issues need a free GitHub account and are public, so leave out personal information and private media.

You can also email [ray@dcha.xyz](mailto:ray@dcha.xyz), for example if you have no GitHub account or your question is
private.

## What to include

- The app version, shown in **Settings**, and where you installed it from: Google Play, the App Store or GitHub.
- Your phone model and its Android or iOS version.
- What you did, what you expected and what happened instead.
- For a video: its resolution, frame rate, whether it is HDR, and the camera that recorded it.

## Logs

To include logs, tap the version number in **Settings** ten times to unlock the developer tools, then reproduce the
problem: the app writes its detailed log only while they are on. Use **Save logs** to keep it:

- **Android:** saved files go to `Download/AquaRestore-analysis`.
- **iPhone:** saved files go to the Files app, under On My iPhone › AquaRestore.

Logs can include the names of your files, and analysis pictures show your media at full quality. Look through them
before you attach them to a public issue. The tools stay on until the next app update, or until you switch them off
in Settings.

## Common questions

**The colors look too strong or wrong.** Lower **Restore intensity**. Clips that mix scenes, very dark footage and
strong colored lights may need a lower intensity, and colors that the water absorbed completely cannot be fully
recovered.

**An export failed.** The phone's encoder may not support the source's codec or size. Choose **Custom** with H.264
and a smaller resolution.

**An export stopped on my iPhone.** Keep AquaRestore open while it exports: leaving the app or locking the screen
pauses the export until you return. Android keeps exporting in the background.

**How do I update the app?** The Google Play and App Store versions update through their stores. The GitHub version
shows **Update now** in **Settings** when a new release is available.

**How do I remove my referral records?** Open an issue with your referral code, shown in **Settings**. Removal can
take away restored unlocks and referral credit.

## Privacy

Your photos and videos are processed on your device and are not sent to the developer. Read the full
[privacy policy](PRIVACY.md).
