# AquaRestore privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

AquaRestore restores the colors of underwater videos and photos. It does not collect, sell or share any personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

## Your photos, videos and music

AquaRestore reads the photos, videos and music you open in the app in order to show, analyze and edit them, and saves the results to your device. All processing happens on the device. Your media is never uploaded.

## Media access

Android asks for permission before the app's library can show your photos, videos and music. You can allow access to selected items only, and change this at any time in the system settings.

## Network

- **Google Play version:** does not contact any server itself. Updates are handled by Google Play.
- **Version downloaded from GitHub:** connects only to GitHub, to check for a newer version of the app and download it when you choose to update. The request contains none of your media or settings; like any web request, it shows GitHub your IP address.

## Settings

Your language and theme choices are stored on the device only.

## Children

AquaRestore is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published in this file, with a new date above. Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/daocha/AquaRestore/issues.
